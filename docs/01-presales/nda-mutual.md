## Background

The Parties wish to discuss a possible engagement for the design, development
and delivery of an inventory management system (the "**Purpose**"). Each Party
may disclose confidential information to the other for that Purpose. This
Agreement sets out how that information is to be treated.

---

## 1. Confidential Information

1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means any information disclosed by one Party
(the "**Discloser**") to the other (the "**Recipient**") in connection with the
Purpose, whether before or after the date of this Agreement, in any form -
written, oral, electronic, visual or observed on site - and whether or not
marked confidential.

1.2 It includes, without limitation:

**Where the Client is the Discloser:** supplier names, agreed prices and
payment terms; purchase volumes; recipes, portion sizes and yields; stock
levels and stock valuations; wastage and shrinkage figures; staff names, roles,
wages and disciplinary matters; sales, turnover and margins; business plans and
expansion plans; and anything observed by TriniphiX personnel while on the
Client's premises.

**Where TriniphiX is the Discloser:** source code, database schemas, system
architecture and design documents; algorithms and business-rule logic;
development methodology, test approach and internal tooling; pricing structure,
rate cards and cost basis; and the identity and terms of TriniphiX's other
clients.

1.3 Confidential Information does **not** include information that the
Recipient can show:

(a) was already lawfully in its possession without an obligation of confidence
before disclosure;
(b) is or becomes public through no act or omission of the Recipient;
(c) is lawfully received from a third party free to disclose it; or
(d) was independently developed by the Recipient without use of or reference to
the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 2. Obligations

2.1 The Recipient shall:

(a) keep the Confidential Information secret and use at least the same degree
of care it uses for its own confidential information, and in no event less than
a reasonable degree of care;
(b) use it solely for the Purpose;
(c) not disclose it to any third party without the Discloser's prior written
consent; and
(d) disclose it within its own organisation only to those directors, employees
and professional advisers who need it for the Purpose, and only where those
persons are bound by obligations of confidence no less strict than these.

2.2 The Recipient remains liable for any breach by a person to whom it
discloses Confidential Information under clause 2.1(d).

2.3 The Recipient shall notify the Discloser in writing without undue delay,
and in any event within **three (3) business days**, on becoming aware of any
unauthorised disclosure, loss or misuse of Confidential Information.

## 3. Permitted disclosure

The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by
law, by a court of competent jurisdiction, or by a regulator, provided it
gives the Discloser as much prior written notice as is lawfully permitted so
that the Discloser may seek protective relief, and discloses only the minimum
required.

## 4. No licence, no obligation to proceed

4.1 Nothing in this Agreement transfers or licenses any intellectual property.
All Confidential Information remains the property of the Discloser.

4.2 Nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to enter into any further
agreement, to disclose any particular information, or to proceed with the
Purpose.

## 5. Return and destruction

On written request, and in any event on termination of discussions, the
Recipient shall within **fourteen (14) days** return or securely destroy all
Confidential Information in its possession, including copies, and confirm in
writing that it has done so. The Recipient may retain one copy to the extent
required by law or by its internal backup and archiving systems, which remains
subject to this Agreement for as long as it is retained.

## 6. Non-solicitation

During the term of this Agreement and for **twelve (12) months** after it ends,
neither Party shall knowingly solicit for employment any employee of the other
Party with whom it has had material contact in connection with the Purpose.
This does not restrict general advertising not targeted at such employees, nor
the employment of a person who responds to it.

## 7. Term

7.1 This Agreement takes effect on the date first written above and continues
for **two (2) years**.

7.2 The confidentiality obligations in clause 2 survive termination and
continue for **three (3) years** from the date of disclosure of the information
in question, or for as long as the information remains a trade secret under
applicable law, whichever is longer.

## 8. Remedies

The Parties agree that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy for breach
of this Agreement, and that the Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief
in addition to any other remedy available to it.

## 9. General

9.1 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement between the
Parties on its subject matter and supersedes any prior understanding on it.

9.2 **Variation.** No variation is effective unless in writing and signed by
both Parties.

9.3 **No waiver.** A failure or delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of
it.

9.4 **Severance.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the rest
continues in full force.

9.5 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's
prior written consent.

9.6 **Governing law and jurisdiction.** This Agreement is governed by the laws
of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, and the Parties submit to
the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Sri Lanka.

9.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including
by scanned or electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of
which together form one agreement.
