## 1. Structure of this agreement

1.1 This Agreement sets out the terms that govern all services TriniphiX
provides to the Client. It does not itself commit either party to any
particular work.

1.2 Specific work is commissioned under a **Statement of Work** ("SOW") signed
by both parties. Each SOW is governed by this Agreement and forms part of it.

1.3 The following documents form part of this Agreement:

| Annexure | Document |
|---|---|
| A | Statement of Work (one per engagement) |
| B | Payment Schedule / Milestone Plan |
| C | Change Request Procedure |
| D | Software Licence Agreement |
| E | Data Processing Agreement |
| F | Service Level Agreement (from acceptance) |

1.4 **Order of precedence.** Where documents conflict, they take precedence in
this order: (a) a signed Change Request; (b) the signed SRS; (c) the SOW;
(d) this Agreement; (e) all other annexures; (f) the Proposal. The Proposal
ranks last: it is a sales document and is superseded by everything agreed
after it.

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Acceptance** | Signature by the Client of the Delivery & Acceptance Certificate, or Deemed Acceptance under clause 7.5 |
| **Business Day** | Monday to Friday excluding public holidays in Sri Lanka |
| **Client Materials** | Data, content, credentials, hardware and access provided by the Client |
| **Defect** | A failure of the Deliverables to conform in a material respect to the signed SRS |
| **Deliverables** | The software, documentation and other items specified in a SOW |
| **SRS** | The Software Requirements Specification, once signed by both parties |
| **Warranty Period** | 90 days from Acceptance |

## 3. Services

3.1 TriniphiX shall provide the services and Deliverables described in each SOW
with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with good industry practice.

3.2 TriniphiX shall use personnel with the necessary skills and may, on notice
to the Client, substitute personnel of equivalent competence.

3.3 TriniphiX may subcontract with the Client's prior written consent, not to be
unreasonably withheld, and remains responsible for its subcontractors' acts and
omissions.

3.4 Dates in a SOW are estimates unless expressly stated to be fixed. Where a
date is fixed, it is conditional on the Client meeting its obligations under
clause 4.

## 4. Client obligations

4.1 The Client shall:

(a) appoint one individual with authority to approve deliverables, decisions
and Change Requests, and notify TriniphiX in writing of any change to that
appointment;
(b) provide Client Materials, access, information and decisions in a timely
manner;
(c) make staff available for requirements gathering, User Acceptance Testing
and training as set out in the SOW;
(d) provide devices, premises network connectivity and physical access;
(e) respond to any request for review, approval or information within
**five (5) Business Days**; and
(f) ensure Client Materials do not infringe any third party's rights.

4.2 **Client delay.** If the Client fails to meet an obligation under clause
4.1 and this delays TriniphiX:

(a) timelines are extended by the period of delay plus a reasonable period for
remobilisation;
(b) TriniphiX may invoice for work performed and for reasonable standby costs;
and
(c) TriniphiX is not liable for any resulting failure to meet a date.

4.3 TriniphiX shall notify the Client in writing when it considers clause 4.2
to be engaged, identifying the obligation and the delay. Delay not notified in
the weekly status report is not relied upon later.

## 5. Change control

5.1 Any change to the scope, Deliverables, timeline or price must be agreed
under the Change Request Procedure (Annexure C) and recorded on a Change
Request Form signed by both parties.

5.2 **No verbal changes.** No instruction, request or agreement made verbally,
by email, by message or in a meeting varies a SOW. This protects both parties.

5.3 TriniphiX is not obliged to begin work on a change before the Change
Request Form is signed, and shall not be treated as having accepted a change by
beginning work on it.

## 6. Fees, invoicing and payment

6.1 Fees, milestones and the payment schedule are set out in Annexure B.

6.2 Unless stated otherwise, engagements are **fixed price** for the scope in
the signed SRS.

6.3 All amounts are exclusive of VAT and any other applicable tax, which is
added at the prevailing rate.

6.4 Invoices are payable within **[FILL: fourteen (14)] days** of the invoice
date.

6.5 **Late payment.** Overdue amounts carry interest at **[FILL: 1.5]% per
month** or part month. Where an invoice is more than **[FILL: thirty (30)]
days** overdue, TriniphiX may, on **seven (7) days'** written notice, suspend
services and withhold Deliverables until payment is received. Suspension under
this clause extends all timelines and does not constitute breach by TriniphiX.

6.6 The Client may withhold payment of a disputed portion of an invoice only if
it notifies TriniphiX in writing within **seven (7) days** of the invoice date,
setting out the grounds. The undisputed balance remains payable.

6.7 Reasonable out-of-pocket expenses agreed in advance in writing are
reimbursed at cost.

6.8 Third-party costs - hosting, domain names, object storage, certificates -
are either paid directly by the Client, or recharged by TriniphiX at cost plus
the margin stated in the SOW.

## 7. Acceptance

7.1 TriniphiX delivers each milestone and notifies the Client in writing.

7.2 The Client has **[FILL: ten (10)] Business Days** from delivery to test the
milestone against the signed SRS and either accept it or give written notice of
Defects.

7.3 A notice of Defects must identify each Defect by reference to the SRS
requirement it fails. A preference, or a requirement not in the signed SRS, is
not a Defect - it is a Change Request.

7.4 TriniphiX shall correct notified Defects within a reasonable period and
resubmit, whereupon a further acceptance period of the same length applies to
the corrected items only.

7.5 **Deemed acceptance.** A milestone is deemed accepted if the Client:

(a) does not give written notice of Defects within the acceptance period; or
(b) uses the Deliverables in live operation for its own business purposes.

7.6 Final Acceptance is recorded on the Delivery & Acceptance Certificate and
triggers the final milestone invoice and the start of the Warranty Period.

## 8. Warranty

8.1 TriniphiX warrants that for **ninety (90) days** from Acceptance the
Deliverables will conform in all material respects to the signed SRS.

8.2 The Client's sole remedy under this warranty is that TriniphiX will, at its
own cost, correct any Defect notified during the Warranty Period.

8.3 The warranty does not cover:

(a) modification of the Deliverables by anyone other than TriniphiX;
(b) use contrary to documentation or training;
(c) faults in Client Materials, Client-provided data, hardware, network or
third-party services;
(d) failure caused by the Client's hosting environment where the Client has
elected to host;
(e) requests for new or changed functionality; or
(f) faults arising from the Client's failure to apply updates supplied by
TriniphiX.

8.4 Except as stated in this clause, all warranties, conditions and terms
implied by statute or common law are excluded to the fullest extent permitted
by law.

## 9. Intellectual property

9.1 **Pre-existing IP.** Each party retains ownership of all intellectual
property it owned before this Agreement or develops independently of it.

9.2 **TriniphiX Background IP.** TriniphiX retains ownership of its
pre-existing frameworks, libraries, tools, patterns and know-how, including
where they are incorporated into the Deliverables ("**Background IP**").

9.3 **Deliverables.** Ownership of the intellectual property in the bespoke
elements of the Deliverables is dealt with in the Software Licence Agreement
(Annexure D). **Nothing transfers to the Client until all sums due under the
relevant SOW have been paid in full.**

9.4 **Licence to Background IP.** On full payment, TriniphiX grants the Client
a perpetual, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use its Background IP
solely as embedded in the Deliverables and solely for the Client's internal
business purposes.

9.5 **Client Materials.** The Client retains ownership of all Client Materials
and grants TriniphiX a licence to use them for the purpose of providing the
services.

9.6 **Client data.** All operational data entered into the system is and
remains the Client's property. On request, and at any time during or after this
Agreement, TriniphiX shall provide a complete export of that data in a
documented, non-proprietary format within **[FILL: ten (10)] Business Days**.

9.7 TriniphiX may describe the engagement in general terms and use the Client's
name and logo as a reference, subject to the Client's prior written approval of
the wording, not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 10. Confidentiality

10.1 The confidentiality obligations in the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement
dated [FILL] are incorporated into this Agreement and continue for the term of
this Agreement and **three (3) years** thereafter.

10.2 Where this Agreement and that NDA conflict, this Agreement prevails.

## 11. Data protection

11.1 Where TriniphiX processes personal data on the Client's behalf, it does so
as a processor and the Client as controller, on the terms of the Data
Processing Agreement (Annexure E).

11.2 Both parties shall comply with the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of
2022 and any other applicable data protection law.

## 12. Liability

12.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits either party's liability for death or
personal injury caused by its negligence, for fraud or fraudulent
misrepresentation, or for any other liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

12.2 Subject to clause 12.1, neither party is liable to the other for loss of
profit, loss of revenue, loss of anticipated savings, loss of business
opportunity, loss of goodwill, or any indirect or consequential loss, however
arising.

12.3 Subject to clauses 12.1 and 12.2, each party's total aggregate liability
arising out of or in connection with this Agreement and all SOWs, whether in
contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty or otherwise,
is limited to **the total fees paid by the Client under the SOW giving rise to
the claim in the twelve (12) months preceding the event**.

12.4 **Data loss.** TriniphiX is not liable for loss or corruption of Client
data except where caused by its own negligence and where the Client has
complied with the backup arrangements set out in the SOW or SLA. Where
TriniphiX operates the backups, this exclusion does not apply to a failure of
those backups caused by TriniphiX's negligence.

12.5 The Client acknowledges that the system's value depends on the accuracy of
data entered into it, including item definitions and pack conversions confirmed
during Phase 0, and that TriniphiX is not liable for consequences arising from
inaccurate data supplied or entered by the Client.

12.6 No claim may be brought more than **twelve (12) months** after the party
bringing it became aware, or ought reasonably to have become aware, of the
circumstances giving rise to it.

## 13. Term and termination

13.1 This Agreement begins on the date first written above and continues until
terminated under this clause.

13.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW on **thirty (30)
days'** written notice, save that neither may terminate a SOW for convenience
between the start of User Acceptance Testing and Acceptance.

13.3 Either party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other:

(a) commits a material breach that is not remediable, or that is remediable and
is not remedied within **fourteen (14) days** of written notice;
(b) fails to pay an undisputed invoice within **thirty (30) days** of a written
demand; or
(c) becomes insolvent, enters liquidation, has a receiver appointed, or ceases
to carry on business.

13.4 **On termination:**

(a) the Client shall pay for all work performed and all costs reasonably
committed up to the effective date;
(b) TriniphiX shall deliver all work in progress, source code, documentation
and credentials for the parts paid for;
(c) each party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information in
accordance with the NDA; and
(d) TriniphiX shall provide the Client's complete data export under clause 9.6
before deleting anything.

13.5 **Termination for the Client's convenience during the build** additionally
requires payment of the milestone in progress in full.

13.6 Clauses 6 (in respect of sums accrued), 9, 10, 11, 12, 14 and 15 survive
termination.

## 14. Non-solicitation

Neither party shall, during the term and for **twelve (12) months** after it
ends, knowingly solicit for employment any employee of the other with whom it
has had material contact under this Agreement. General advertising not targeted
at such employees is not a breach.

## 15. General

15.1 **Force majeure.** Neither party is liable for failure or delay caused by
an event beyond its reasonable control, including natural disaster, war, civil
unrest, epidemic, nationwide power failure or nationwide failure of internet
infrastructure. The affected party shall notify the other promptly and use
reasonable efforts to mitigate. If the event continues for more than
**sixty (60) days**, either party may terminate the affected SOW without
liability.

15.3 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement and its annexures are the entire
agreement between the parties and supersede all prior discussions,
representations and proposals. Neither party has relied on any statement not
set out in it. This clause does not exclude liability for fraudulent
misrepresentation.

15.4 **Variation.** No variation is effective unless in writing and signed by
an authorised representative of each party.

15.5 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign or transfer this Agreement
without the other's prior written consent, save that either may assign to a
successor of substantially the whole of its business on written notice.

15.6 **Independent contractors.** Nothing creates a partnership, joint venture
or employment relationship.

15.7 **No third-party rights.** No person other than the parties has any right
to enforce any term of this Agreement.

15.8 **Severance.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, it is
severed and the remainder continues in force.

15.9 **Waiver.** No failure or delay in exercising a right is a waiver of it.

15.10 **Dispute resolution.** The parties shall first attempt to resolve any
dispute by negotiation between their nominated representatives within
**fourteen (14) days**, and failing that between senior management within a
further **fourteen (14) days**. If unresolved, the dispute shall be referred to
arbitration in Colombo under the Arbitration Act, No. 11 of 1995, before a
single arbitrator, in English. Nothing prevents either party from seeking
urgent injunctive relief from a court.

15.11 **Governing law and jurisdiction.** This Agreement is governed by the laws
of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, and subject to clause 15.10
the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Sri Lanka.

15.12 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts,
including by scanned or electronic signature.
