### Why the split is weighted this way

P1 at 30% covers the team being reserved and the requirements work, which is
the phase where most of the thinking happens and none of the visible output
does. P5 at 15% is deliberately meaningful - it keeps TriniphiX motivated
through cutover and training, which is where projects are abandoned by
suppliers who have already been paid.

## 7. Late payment

7.1 Overdue amounts carry interest at **[FILL: 1.5]% per month** or part month
from the due date until paid.

7.2 Where an undisputed invoice is more than **[FILL: thirty (30)] days**
overdue, TriniphiX may suspend work and withhold Deliverables on seven days'
written notice. Suspension extends every subsequent date in the milestone plan
by the period of suspension plus a reasonable period for remobilisation.

7.3 **Intellectual property in the Deliverables does not transfer until all
sums due have been paid in full** (MSA clause 9.3).

## 8. Disputed invoices

8.1 To dispute part of an invoice the Client must notify TriniphiX in writing
within **seven (7) days** of the invoice date, identifying the disputed amount
and the grounds.

8.2 The undisputed balance remains payable on the original due date.

8.3 The parties shall resolve the dispute under MSA clause 15.10.

## 9. Effect of Client delay on payment

Where a milestone trigger is delayed by a Client dependency (SOW §6), and
TriniphiX has notified that delay in a weekly status report, TriniphiX may
invoice that milestone **thirty (30) days after the date the milestone would
otherwise have been reached**, provided the work on TriniphiX's side is
complete and available for demonstration.

This exists so that a project stalled waiting for the Client's item master does
not sit unpaid indefinitely. It cannot be used where the delay is TriniphiX's.
